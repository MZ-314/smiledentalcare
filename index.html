Meet the Doctor

BDS Dental Surgeon 8 Years Experience

Dr. S Al Jakaria

Founder & Chief Dental Surgeon, Smile Dental Care

Dr. S Al Jakaria is a dedicated and compassionate dental surgeon with over 8 years of hands-on clinical experience in general, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry. Armed with a BDS degree, Dr. Jakaria founded Smile Dental Care with a singular vision — to make exceptional dental care accessible to every patient in Guwahati.

Renowned for his calm, reassuring chairside manner, Dr. Jakaria takes genuine time with each patient before recommending any treatment. He pursues continuing dental education regularly, keeping himself at the cutting edge of orthodontics, implantology, and aesthetic dentistry. Whether attending to a nervous child or performing a complex restorative procedure, his commitment to painless, precise, and honest care never wavers.
